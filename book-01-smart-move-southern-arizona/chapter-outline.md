## Notes on Structure

- **Six-part arc** carries the reader from "why move here" through buying, selling, downsizing, and finally "living well" — so the book serves relocation buyers, retirees, downsizers, and sellers without feeling like four separate books stitched together.
- Each chapter is written to double as **standalone content**: a blog post, a short video script, a seminar talk, or a social carousel, so the book seeds a year-plus of marketing material.
- Chapters 1–4 and 18–21 (lifestyle-focused) are the most shareable/lead-magnet-friendly content; chapters 6–13 (transaction-focused) are where authority and expertise are most demonstrated.
- Checklists in Chapter 24 can be pulled out as free opt-in lead magnets ahead of the book's release.
